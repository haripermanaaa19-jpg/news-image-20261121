The lead is the Russian missile strike on Poland, confirmed by Prime Minister Tusk as NATO responds, with the day's fresh strikes on Ukraine alongside it. Around it: the deepening tech sell-off that pushed the S&P 500 into correction, Cyclone Akvan's landfall on the Odisha coast, the hack of Austria's Foreign Ministry website, Nikhil Gupta's 19-year US sentence, the French police officers sentenced over the beating of Michel Zecler, the EU's €1 billion military aid package for Ukraine with Estonia's border closure, and KL Rahul completing his SENA set of Test centuries — with a markets strip along the foot. The front page is stored in this repository as `entry.png`, and `main.ts` checks it is a whole PNG or JPEG within the 1 MiB limit before copying it to `/output/entry.png`.
